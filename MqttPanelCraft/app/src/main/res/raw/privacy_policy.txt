PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: 2026-09-20
App: MqttPanelCraft
Developer and privacy contact: Spec127 / niceboat919@gmail.com

Local data and connections
We do not operate an account or project-storage backend. Projects, broker addresses, credentials, topics, layouts, chosen images, web code and received values are stored on your device. MQTT messages go directly to the broker you configure. A web component may load a third-party site, whose operator can receive network and browser data under its own policy. Local tutorial messages stay in the local simulator.

This version uses cleartext MQTT connections, not an implemented TLS connection mode. Use trusted networks or an independently secured tunnel; do not send secrets over public test brokers. Brokers and websites have their own security, retention and deletion practices.

Advertising and privacy choices
The free app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the User Messaging Platform. Depending on settings and consent, Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device/app identifiers, diagnostics and advertising interactions. Non-personalized ads do not mean no data processing. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
An age band (not a date of birth) is stored locally to apply advertising restrictions. Unknown age does not permit ad requests. Under-18 settings use conservative restricted, non-personalized requests. Where required, the consent form and About > Advertising privacy options let you manage choices. Ads are not requested when the app cannot establish the required permission. The app is designed primarily for users aged 15 and above; age handling is not parental consent or proof of legal eligibility.

Purchases
Google Play handles payment details; we do not receive payment-card numbers. The app processes signed purchase information, entitlement status and tokens for verification and acknowledgement. Pending acknowledgement work and verified entitlement are kept locally and excluded from Android backup. Reopening the app while online reconciles purchases. Premium removes ads and save waits; Google Play may still process purchase data. Google services follow their own policies.

Retention, backups and deletion
Local project data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall. Android backup/device transfer may include projects and credentials, so treat backups and exported JSON as sensitive. Deleting the app does not delete copies you exported, Google purchase records or data held by your broker or websites. Use the relevant provider's controls for that data.
Local crash reports may contain diagnostic text and are not automatically sent to us. You can inspect, clear or voluntarily share them from About; remove credentials and payloads before sharing.

Permissions
Internet/network access supports MQTT, web content, ads and billing. Selected-image/storage permissions support imported assets. Foreground-service and notification permissions support the optional persistent MQTT session. Advertising consent is separate from Android permissions.

Requests and changes
Contact niceboat919@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion questions regarding information you send to us. We cannot remotely erase projects held only on your device. Google processing may occur outside your country; applicable rights depend on your location. This notice does not limit statutory privacy or consumer rights. Updates change the effective date. The published store policy must match this notice.
