PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 2026-01-26

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how MqttPanelCraft ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data Collection and Use
We do not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from our users. The App operates primarily as a local tool for creating MQTT dashboards.

3. App Permissions
- INTERNET: Connect to MQTT brokers and load ads.
- ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Check network connectivity.
- READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Load optional panel background images.

4. Third-Party Services
Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may collect data, such as the Advertising ID, to serve ads. Its use is governed by Google’s applicable privacy policy and terms.

5. MQTT Data
Your MQTT configuration and payload data are processed locally on your device or sent directly to the MQTT broker you specify. We do not have access to your MQTT data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Please review it periodically for changes.
